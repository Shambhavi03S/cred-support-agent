# Fraud-Dispute Resolution Process
topic: fraud-dispute resolution process

A customer who notices an unauthorised transaction should report it to Cred support or block the card in the app immediately, because reporting within three working days limits the customer liability to zero. The dispute is logged with a reference number, the card or account is temporarily blocked, and the case is sent to the fraud-review team. A provisional credit is given within ten working days while the investigation continues, and the final outcome is shared within ninety days. Loan applications flagged for fraud review are held until the team clears them.
