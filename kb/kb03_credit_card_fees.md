# Credit-Card Fee Structure
topic: credit-card fee structure

The Cred Select credit card carries a joining fee of INR 999 and an annual fee of INR 999, which is waived when yearly spends cross INR 1,50,000. Interest on unpaid balances is charged at 3.6 percent per month, and cash withdrawals attract a fee of 2.5 percent of the amount, subject to a minimum of INR 300. A late-payment fee applies when the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, ranging from INR 100 for dues up to INR 500 to INR 1,000 for dues above INR 10,000. Foreign-currency transactions include a mark-up fee of 3.5 percent.
