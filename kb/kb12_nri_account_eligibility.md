# NRI-Account Eligibility
topic: NRI-account eligibility

Non-Resident Indians and Persons of Indian Origin can open NRE, NRO and FCNR(B) accounts with Cred. An NRE account holds money remitted from abroad in foreign currency, which is converted to rupees, and its balance and interest are fully repatriable. An NRO account manages income earned in India such as rent or dividends and has limited repatriation. Applicants must submit a valid passport, visa or residence permit, overseas address proof and a PAN card or Form 60.
