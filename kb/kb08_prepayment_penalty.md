# Prepayment-Penalty Rules
topic: prepayment-penalty rules

Cred does not charge any prepayment or foreclosure penalty on floating-rate loans taken by individual borrowers. Fixed-rate loans attract a penalty of 2 percent of the principal outstanding at the time of the prepayment. Part-prepayments must be at least equal to three EMIs and can be made once per quarter. After a foreclosure request, the bank issues a no-dues certificate and releases the original documents within 15 working days.
