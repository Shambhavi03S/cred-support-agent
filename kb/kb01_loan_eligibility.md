# Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type
topic: loan eligibility criteria by loan type

Cred Personal Loans are open to salaried applicants aged 21 to 60 with a minimum monthly income of INR 25,000 and at least one year of work history. Home Loans require a minimum monthly income of INR 40,000, an applicant age of 21 to 65 at maturity, and a property that passes legal and technical verification. Auto Loans need a minimum income of INR 20,000 per month and finance up to 85 percent of the on-road price of the vehicle. Education Loans are granted to students admitted to a recognised institution, and a co-applicant such as a parent must be added. Business Loans require at least two years of business vintage and two years of filed income-tax returns.
