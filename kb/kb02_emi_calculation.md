# EMI Calculation Rules
topic: EMI calculation rules

The equated monthly instalment is computed with the reducing-balance formula EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and by 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments. Early instalments carry a larger interest share, and the principal share grows each month as the outstanding balance falls. For example, a loan of INR 5,00,000 at 12 percent per annum over 36 months has an EMI of about INR 16,607. Customers can see the full amortisation schedule in the app after disbursal.
