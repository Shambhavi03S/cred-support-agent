# Joint-Account Rules
topic: joint-account rules

A joint account can be opened by two or more individuals, and the operating mode is chosen as either-or-survivor, former-or-survivor or jointly. In an either-or-survivor account any holder can transact alone, whereas in a jointly-operated account every holder must sign. All joint holders must complete KYC, and the statements are sent to the first holder. After the death of a holder, the survivor can continue to operate the account once the death certificate is submitted.
