# Loan Application Lifecycle and Statuses
topic: loan application status definitions

A loan application moves through five statuses: Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Rejected and Disbursed. Submitted means the application has been received and is awaiting document checks, and Under Review means credit and verification teams are assessing it. Approved applications get a sanction letter, and the funds are released after the agreement is signed, at which point the status becomes Disbursed. Rejected applications may be re-submitted after 90 days, and the reason for rejection is communicated to the customer.
