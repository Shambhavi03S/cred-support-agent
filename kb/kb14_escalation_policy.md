# Escalation Policy for Applications
topic: escalation policy for ageing or fraud-flagged applications

Support staff escalate a loan application to a senior officer when its escalation score is 0.70 or higher. The score combines a fraud-review flag, which contributes 0.5, with an ageing signal equal to 0.5 multiplied by days since creation divided by 30. In practice a fraud-flagged application older than about 12 days is escalated, while an unflagged application is only monitored. Escalated cases must receive a first response within one working day.
