# Grievance Redressal and Late Payment
topic: grievance redressal and late payment

Customers can raise a complaint through the app, the toll-free helpline or any branch, and receive an acknowledgement with a complaint number. The first-level support team resolves most complaints within seven working days, after which unresolved cases go to the Nodal Officer. If the customer is not satisfied after thirty days, they may approach the RBI Ombudsman. For late EMI payments, a penal charge of 2 percent per month is levied on the overdue amount, and accounts overdue by more than 90 days are classified as non-performing.
