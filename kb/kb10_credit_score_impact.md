# Credit-Score Impact Factors
topic: credit-score impact factors

Payment history is the most important factor in a credit score, so missed or late EMIs lower it quickly. A high credit-utilisation ratio, meaning more than 30 percent of the card limit in use, also pulls the score down. A long credit history and a healthy mix of secured and unsecured loans help, whereas many loan applications in a short time create several hard enquiries that reduce the score. Defaults, settlements and write-offs stay on the credit report for years.
