# Interest-Rate Slabs
topic: interest-rate slabs

Interest rates depend on the loan type and the borrower credit score. Home Loans start at 8.5 percent per annum for scores of 750 and above, rising to 9.5 percent for scores between 700 and 749. Personal Loans range from 11 to 16 percent, Auto Loans from 9 to 12 percent, Education Loans from 9.5 to 12.5 percent and Business Loans from 12 to 18 percent. Applicants with scores below 650 may be declined or offered a higher slab at the bank discretion.
