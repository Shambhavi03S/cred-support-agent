# Account-Closure Process
topic: account-closure process

To close a savings account the customer submits a closure request through the app or a branch after clearing all dues and linked loans. Unused cheque books and debit cards must be surrendered or cancelled, and any standing instructions and auto-debits are cancelled by the bank. The remaining balance is paid to another account of the customer by transfer or demand draft within seven working days. Accounts closed within 30 days of opening attract no charge, while closure after that has a service fee of INR 500.
