# Minimum-Balance Requirements
topic: minimum-balance requirements

A Cred Regular savings account needs an average monthly balance of INR 5,000 in metro and urban branches and INR 2,000 in semi-urban and rural branches. The Premium account requires an average monthly balance of INR 50,000. Salary accounts and basic savings accounts for Jan Dhan customers carry no minimum balance. If the average falls short, a non-maintenance charge of 5 percent of the shortfall, capped at INR 500 a month, is deducted.
