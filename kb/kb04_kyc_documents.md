# KYC Document Requirements
topic: KYC document requirements

Every borrower must complete Know Your Customer verification before a loan is disbursed. Identity can be proved with a PAN card together with an officially valid document such as Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence. Address proof can be an Aadhaar, passport, utility bill not older than three months, or a rent agreement. Salaried applicants also submit the last three salary slips and six months of bank statements, while business applicants add GST registration and income-tax returns.
